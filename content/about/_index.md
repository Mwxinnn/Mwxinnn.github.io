# Wenxin (Wendy) Ma <span class="name-zh" lang="zh">马雯芯</span>

I’m Wendy, a **first-year PhD student in Computer Science at The University of Texas at Austin**, advised by Prof. [Philipp Krähenbühl](https://www.philkr.net/). My research spans **computer vision** and **medical image analysis**, with an interest in active exploration and spatial and temporal reasoning.

I joined the [MIRACLE Lab](https://miracle.ustc.edu.cn/main.html) at the **University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)** for my master’s studies in 2023, advised by Prof. [S. Kevin Zhou](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8eNm2GMAAAAJ&hl=en) and Prof. [Zihang Jiang](https://zihangjiang.github.io/). In April 2025, I joined **Johns Hopkins University** as a visiting scholar, working with Prof. [Alan Yuille](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FJ-huxgAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Jieneng Chen](https://beckschen.github.io/).

Previously, I received my bachelor’s degree from the Zhiyuan Honored Program at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**.
