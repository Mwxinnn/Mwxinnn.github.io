 My research focuses on AI in general / medical applications:
 - **Efficient and Robust Methods** to identify importance and remove redundancy, enabling efficient training and robust inference. For example, I interest in a label-efficient application - **Anomaly Detection** - with high accuracy ([WACV25'](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12295)) and high generalization ability ([CVPR25'](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06661)).
 - **Vision Language Model** as an adaptable and powerful foundation for various tasks. I interest in understanding the decision process of vision and language models to investigate new interaction pipeline between text and visual domains, unleashing the potential of LLMs in vision tasks.
